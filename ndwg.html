The Early Web at Notre Dame

Between 2005 and 2011, the Notre Dame Web Group (NDWG) was instrumental in shaping the University's online presence. During this era, web design transitioned from table-based layouts to CSS-driven standards. The following gallery showcases original screenshots of departmental and official University sites designed by the group during this formative period.
