Quick Rules
  • Public data is low-risk campus info.
  • Internal is ND-only but not very sensitive.
  • Sensitive could seriously harm ND if misused (FERPA records, salaries, etc.).
  • Restricted is extremely private (SSNs, credit cards, biometric data) and is never allowed in AI tools.

💡 If a tool is approved for Sensitive data, it’s also okay for Internal & Public – but still not for Restricted.
