Data: Artificial Analysis model pages and per-provider benchmark pages, retrieved 24 July 2026. Intelligence = Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.1 (GDPval-AA v2, 𝜏³-Banking, Terminal-Bench v2.1, SciCode, Humanity's Last Exam, GPQA Diamond, CritPt, AA-Omniscience, AA-LCR). Caveats: Claude 4.5 Haiku's index is an Artificial Analysis estimate pending independent eval. GLM-5.2's speed and price are medians across 17 providers rather than first-party. Measured speeds drift by a few tokens/sec between snapshots. Cache-hit discounts (up to −90% at Anthropic and Google) are not modelled and would move prompt-heavy workloads toward the cheap corner. Mistral is excluded because its best model scores 30, below every model here; xAI has no genuinely cheap tier with live pricing, so Grok 4.3 stands in.
